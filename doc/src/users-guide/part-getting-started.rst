Getting Started
===============

This part is the way in. The introduction says what Simu5G is, what it models
and at what level of abstraction. The usage chapter covers installation, the
layout of the source tree, and running the example simulations. Building a
Scenario then walks through the construction of a complete simulation, one
step at a time, and points to the chapter that covers each step in depth.

Readers who already know OMNeT++ and INET, and want to see a working setup
first, can go straight to Building a Scenario and come back to the rest of the
guide as the questions arise.
